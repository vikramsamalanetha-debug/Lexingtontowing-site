FREQUENTLY ASKED

Towing questions, answered

The most common things people ask before calling for a tow in Lexington, TN.

How much does towing cost in Lexington, TN?

Pricing is based on a hookup fee plus mileage, and varies by vehicle size (light-duty vs. medium-duty) and distance. Use the instant estimate calculator above for a real quote based on your vehicle and pickup/drop-off locations — final price is always confirmed by dispatch before your truck is sent.

How fast will a tow truck get to me?

Response time depends on where you are — typically 15-20 minutes for downtown, the bypass, or the I-40 corridor, and up to 30 minutes for rural county roads. See the "Response by area" panel above for a full breakdown.

What should I do if I break down on I-40 near Lexington?

Get as far onto the shoulder as safely possible, turn on your hazard lights, and stay inside your vehicle with your seatbelt on if you're in a live traffic lane — it's safer than standing outside near passing traffic. Then call (901) 237-0047 and we'll get a truck routed to your exact location.

Do you tow SUVs, trucks, and vans — or just cars?

We handle both light-duty vehicles (cars, SUVs, half-ton trucks, and vans) and medium-duty vehicles (3/4-ton-and-up pickups and full-size vans). The estimate calculator automatically detects which category your vehicle falls into once you select its year, make, and model.

Are you available nights, weekends, and holidays?

Yes — dispatch is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. Emergency towing doesn't wait for business hours, and neither do we.

What areas around Lexington do you serve?

We cover downtown and the city limits, the north bypass and surrounding suburbs, the I-40 corridor, and rural county roads throughout Henderson County — including Scotts Hill, Sardis, Darden, Wildersville, Reagan, and Chesterfield. We also reach roughly 30 miles beyond the county line, out to Jackson, Henderson, Milan, Parsons, Linden, and Crump. Call to confirm coverage and response time for anywhere outside the immediate county.
