Share Your Pickup Location

Help our driver find you faster by securely sharing your phone's current GPS location.

How it works:
1. Tap Share My Location.
2. Your phone will ask for location permission.
3. Tap Allow.
4. We'll locate you using your GPS coordinates.
Your location is only accessed after you give permission. Lexington Towing & Recovery uses it to assist with your towing or roadside service request.
